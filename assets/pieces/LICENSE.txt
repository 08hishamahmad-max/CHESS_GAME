Chess piece images (assets/pieces/*.png and tools/cburnett_svg/*.svg)

where they are distributed under the GNU GPL v2 or later.

CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/),
GFDL and GPL.

The PNG files were rendered from the unmodified SVGs with
tools/render_pieces.py (256 x 256 pixels).
